Common questions

Who may be suitable for a transplant?

Candidacy depends on the cause of hair loss, donor hair availability, health and goals. It is decided at assessment.

What are the potential risks?

These can include swelling, infection, scarring, shock loss and uneven growth. A clinician will discuss risks specific to you.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Results vary between individuals.
